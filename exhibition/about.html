Please Wait

Curated by Alveera Munshi

Every website I use is built to save me time. The five works in Please Wait all have some kind of technical quirk that forces users to engage for an extended period.

Olia Lialina's My Boyfriend Came Back from the War becomes smaller and smaller in a pixel-sized grid. Every Icon takes forever as well; it counts through a 32-by-32 grid in every black-and-white image it can hold, which the Whitney reckons would take several hundred trillion years, so nobody alive has seen the second row. Summer is a super slow GIF made up of lengthy redirects. The last two are blunter. fallingfalling.com drops keep falling and have no clear end, and The Deep Sea is a LONG scroll.

All of these treat time, and its perception as a common element, from 1996 frames to 2019 infinite scroll.

Sometimes, some of the links keep breaking. Rhizome's restoration, and the Internet Archive is the only reason we can even discuss these works. Otherwise, we might lose them forever.
